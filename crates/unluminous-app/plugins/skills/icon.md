# Making this plugin's icon again

`icon.png` (32 by 32) and `icon-128.png` are drawn by `icon.py` in this folder, with Pillow:
`python icon.py` writes both beside it.

The mark is Lucide's `blocks` (the `LuBlocks` the ticket named) drawn as filled blocks: three in an L in the cyan
`#22B8E6` and the fourth, lifted away to the top right, in the magenta `#D946EF` the other drawn plugin icons use.
The rail button and the Settings page draw the line version from `theme::svg_icon::BLOCKS` instead.
