# Making this plugin's icon again

`icon.png` (32 by 32) and `icon-128.png` are drawn by `icon.py` in this folder, with Pillow:
`python icon.py` writes both beside it. Nothing in it is random, so running it again gives the same two files.

The mark is a page with its top right corner folded over, a magenta heading and four cyan lines of writing,
flat on a transparent background in the cyan `#22B8E6` and magenta `#D946EF` the Spreadsheet, Mermaid, JSON,
Database and Realm icons use. It fills its square as the Spreadsheet icon does (`task-2402`). The picture before
it was a small white page on a navy tile, and on a dark theme the tile vanished and the page read at about half
the size of the icons beside it in the explorer.

The Settings page's icon is not this picture. It is `settings.icon = document`, which `theme::icon::document`
draws in one colour so it follows the window's colours like every other mark there. This picture is the one
the plugins page and the marketplace show, and the one drawn beside a `.docx` in the explorer and on its tab.
